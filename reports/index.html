Safety

Boiler blowdown: Follow your site's blowdown procedure. Wear face shield and gloves. Open the valve slowly; stand to the side. Never leave an open blowdown valve. If you are not trained, don't do it. Call your supervisor.

Chemicals: Read the SDS. Wear goggles and chemical gloves. Never mix chemicals (bleach + acid = toxic gas). Know where the eyewash is.

Tower: Don't stand in tower drift or open the tower without your supervisor's OK. Lock out fans before reaching in.

Hot boiler samples: Use the sample cooler. Sample water can scald.
